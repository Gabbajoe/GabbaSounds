## ⚔️ New in 1.1.0: Let's get melee!

**Made by special request from Tsukimo!**

🗡️ **Swords & axes** · 🔨 **Maces & staves** · 🔪 **Daggers** · 👊 **Fists & unarmed**

Each group gets **6 normal-hit sounds and 4 crit reactions** — **40 new voice clips** in total. Main hand and off hand choose their sounds to match your weapons. Misses and glancing blows get their own shared comments, too!

Fast hits? Your longer crit and miss reactions still get a chance to finish. 🛡️

## ✨ More variety, more personality

- 🎲 **Keep it fresh:** random variants without immediate repeats when alternatives are available.
- 💥 **Make crits count:** separate reactions for normal hits and critical hits.
- 🗣️ **Speak while casting:** mage clips at cast start, channel start or activation, as appropriate — plus impact reactions for damaging spells.
- ❄️ **Enjoy the big spells:** area attacks react once per application; Blizzard reacts to its first damage burst.
- 🔇 **Let your voice take over:** optionally mute covered original sounds with the “spoken sounds only” setting.
- 🎧 **Try before you choose:** previews in the addon and [right here in your browser](https://gabbajoe.github.io/GabbaSounds/).
- 🧭 **One handy minimap button:** left-click on/off, right-click options, drag to reposition.
- 💾 **Make it yours:** choose your pack and save settings per character. No required addon dependencies.

## 🚀 Ready for a little pew pew?

Install GabbaSounds, **fully restart WoW**, then open **`/gws`** or right-click the minimap button to choose your sounds.

**All spoken packs + covered originals muted:**

```text
/gws on
/gws spokenonly on
```

**🎮 WoW Classic Era / Hardcore · 🗣️ German recordings and options interface**

Melee covers ordinary auto attacks. The recordings play on your own client; they aren't broadcast to other players.

For installation details, commands, supported abilities and original-sound muting, see the [📚 usage guide](https://github.com/Gabbajoe/GabbaSounds/blob/main/docs/USAGE.md).
