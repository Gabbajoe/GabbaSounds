**🎧 [Listen to the sound packs](https://gabbajoe.github.io/GabbaSounds/) · 📦 [GitHub downloads](https://github.com/Gabbajoe/GabbaSounds/releases) · 💬 [Ideas & feedback](https://github.com/Gabbajoe/GabbaSounds/issues)**

## 🧙 New in 1.3.0: Minibobsel, time to rebuff!

Four original voice reminders give **Minibobsel the mage** a heads-up when buffs have **two minutes or less remaining**, and a different reminder when they're missing. Intellect/Brilliance covers you and visible mana-using group members; your own Frost, Ice or Mage Armor counts as one armor buff. 💜

**This personal automatic reminder is restricted to a mage named Minibobsel.** Everyone can listen to the four clips in `/gws buffs` or [in the browser](https://gabbajoe.github.io/GabbaSounds/previews/buffs/index.html). Reminders stay on your own screen, share one voice reaction across affected group members and support the higher Noggenfogger voice. No messages are sent to your party or raid.

## ✨ New in 1.4.0: Priests get a voice!

**28 attack clips** for Mind Blast, Shadow Word: Pain, Smite and Holy Nova.
Shadow Word: Pain reacts on application or refresh, without extra comments on
every tick. Holy Nova combines damage targets into one reaction and ignores healing.
**Spoken priest buff reminders** cover Fortitude, Divine Spirit, your own Inner Fire
and optional learned Fear Ward. Group reminders require suitable classes in confirmed
buff range. Shared miss and partial-hit comments complete the pack. 💛

**🌿 Herb hunting with personality:** tooltip alerts for Gromsblood and Black Lotus when you hover a real herb point or the herb itself. Herbalism required; no automatic minimap scanning.

**🎛️ Everything in one place:** a compact, opaque settings window with clear navigation, direct sound selection and previews that keep your active package unchanged. Choose Mage or Priest under buff reminders to hear the matching clips.

## 🐭 Small character, higher voice!

Noggenfogger shrank you? Your GabbaSounds get **four semitones higher** while the shrink buff lasts! Your spoken attacks, mage casts and synthetic magic all join in — with the **same playback duration**. When the buff ends, your normal voice returns. 💜

Your effect changes only **your own sounds**; other players keep their normal voice. Skeleton form and slow fall alone don't trigger it.

The feature is **enabled by default** and can be switched off in `/gws` or with `/gws noggenfogger off`. **Fully restart WoW after updating** to load all 349 pitched variants.

## ✨ More variety, more personality

- 🎲 **Keep it fresh:** random variants without immediate repeats when alternatives are available.
- 💥 **Make crits count:** separate reactions for normal hits and critical hits.
- 🗣️ **Speak while casting:** mage clips at cast start, channel start or activation, as appropriate — plus impact reactions for damaging spells.
- ❄️ **Enjoy the big spells:** area attacks react once per application; Blizzard reacts to its first damage burst.
- 🔇 **Let your voice take over:** optionally mute covered original sounds with the “spoken sounds only” setting.
- 🎧 **Try before you choose:** previews in the addon and [right here in your browser](https://gabbajoe.github.io/GabbaSounds/).
- 🧭 **One handy minimap button:** left-click on/off, right-click options, drag to reposition.
- 💾 **Make it yours:** choose your pack and save settings per character. No required addon dependencies.

## 🚀 Ready for a little pew pew?

Install GabbaSounds, **fully restart WoW**, then open **`/gws`** or right-click the minimap button to choose your sounds.

**All spoken packs + covered originals muted:**

```text
/gws on
/gws spokenonly on
```

**🎮 WoW Classic Era / Hardcore · 🗣️ German recordings and options interface**

Melee covers ordinary auto attacks. The recordings play on your own client; they aren't broadcast to other players.

For installation details, commands, supported abilities and original-sound muting, see the [📚 usage guide](https://github.com/Gabbajoe/GabbaSounds/blob/main/docs/USAGE.md).
