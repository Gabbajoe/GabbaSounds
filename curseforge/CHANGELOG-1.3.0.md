# GabbaSounds 1.3.0

Personal mage buff reminders with four original German voice recordings.

- Added automatic reminders exclusively for a mage named **Minibobsel**. Other characters retain their existing behavior and can preview the recordings.
- Check Arcane Intellect/Brilliance on yourself and visible, connected, living mana-using party or raid members. Any rank and buffs from other mages count; warriors and rogues are excluded.
- Check your own Frost, Ice or Mage Armor as one armor buff category, only after a relevant spell has been learned.
- Play separate recordings for buffs with at most two minutes remaining and for missing/expired buffs. Display personal screen warnings without sending party or raid messages.
- Coalesce group voice reminders, play different reminders in sequence and discard resolved queued warnings. Rebuffing rearms warnings; identical voice reminders have a 30-second minimum interval.
- Wait for aura data after loading screens and stay quiet while dead or on a taxi.
- Added `/gws buffs`, individual reminder switches and four previews in-game and on the listening website.
- Included four matching +4-semitone Noggenfogger variants. The ZIP now contains **698 OGGs**: 223 normal spoken recordings, 126 synthetic sounds and 349 pitched variants. Original WAVs remain private.

Warrior ability recordings are still in preparation. Automatic live herbalism scanning is not part of this release.

**Fully exit and restart WoW after updating** so the client can load all new audio files.

Target client: WoW Classic Era / Hardcore 1.15.9, Interface 11509.
